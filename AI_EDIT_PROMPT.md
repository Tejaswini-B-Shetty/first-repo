# Instructions for an AI editing ROOTLINK

Give the AI the ROOTLINK source ZIP and ask it to work on the project, not just describe changes. Example:

> Please edit this ROOTLINK app. First inspect the included README, data-source manifest, and translation glossary. Preserve the offline-first browser app and its farmer-friendly design. Keep English, Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada in sync. Do not invent farm predictions, weather, yields, buyers, prices, contacts, or verified listings. Make the requested change in the source files, explain what you changed, and give me the updated files or ZIP. If you need a live data source, explain the source, license, and limitations before adding it.

The public app link is for viewing and trying ROOTLINK. It does not grant write access to its source. To make edits, give the AI the downloaded `rootlink-source.zip` (or a copy of the project folder) as an attachment. Each person gets their own browser-local farm data; the app does not sync data between users.
